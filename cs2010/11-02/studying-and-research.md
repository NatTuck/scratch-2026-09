
# Studying and Research (with an LLM helper)

Studying:

- Taking tests?
- Memorization component
- LLM artifacts: Practice tests, practice materials
- Topics are well defined

Research:

- Finding out stuff?
- Less likely to need to memorize anything specific
- Might be safer to rely on LLM more for stuff like summaries?
- More open ended. There's a figuring out what we need to figure
out step.

## Studying Example

- We'll study for a Calc 1 quiz.
- It's chapter 3.5 of our online textbook.
- What's likely to be on the quiz:
  - We'll have to evaluate trig functions.
  - We need to know derivatives of trig functions.
  - We may need to do a simple proof.

## Research Example

We need to write a three page research paper covering the history
of ancient Carthage, with references to primary sources.
