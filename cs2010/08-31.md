## Data on Computers

- Electricity has voltage, current, etc.
  - Components have capacitance, inductance, magnetic fields, etc.
- But it's really easy to distinguish "on" (1) and "off" (0).
- The basic unit of data is a "bit", or "binary digit". A value of
0 or 1.
