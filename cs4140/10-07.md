
## Grange: What Are the Game Loops?

Fast: Planting, Watering, Growing Crops

- Action: Plant and grow seed
- Feedback: Seed grows
- Reward: Pick crop, sell or replant
- Motivation: Make money for the casino, buy booze

Medium: Upgrading the Farm

- Farm equipment
- Automation and farm workers, autoharvesting, be like Cookie Clicker

- Action: Add upgrades
- Feedback: They install
- Reward: More money faster
- Motivation: Stuff goes faster, scaling

Long loop:

- Action: Long term drinking and gambling
- Feedback: Blackouts
- Reward: Liver disease
- Motivation: Social commentary

Casino:

- Casino stuff

Crops:

- Make your own beer, wine, bathtub gin, etc.
