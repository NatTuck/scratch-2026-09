Convert the number 3240 in base 5 to base 9.

Through base 10:

- To base 10: Figure out the places, then multiply and add.
  - 5^3 = 125, 5^2 = 25, 5^1 = 5, 5^0 = 1
  - So `3*125 + 2*25 + 4*5 + 0` = 375 + 50 + 20 + 0 = 445
- To base 9: Find the largest power of *9* that goes into 445.
  - 9^2 = 81, 9^1 = 9, 9^0 = 1
  - `5 * 81 = 405, leaving 40; 4 * 9 = 36, leaving 0; 4 1's`
  - = 544 base 9
