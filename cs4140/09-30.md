
## Planning

Sounds like Agile is hostile to planning.

Yes, Agile avoids overplanning.

But we still have goals and deadlines.

Rather than full up front planning, we try to
figure out those goals and what it would take to
hit our deadlines.

Then we have to make continuous adjustments to get
good outcomes.

And hitting goals by deadlines necessarily means
either adjusting the goals or the deadlines when
things go wrong.

### Example

Imagine we were trying to build an E-Commerce website by
February.

Goals:

- User logins
- Payments and payment confirmations
- We need to trick the user into thinking online payments
are "secure".
- Pretty UI
- Products
- Shopping cart workflow

Deadlines:

- Everything done and tested by February for launch.
- Feature complete in December
- Minimal prototype (product pages, product page editing, shopping cart,
users, fake checkout) by Nov 1st.
- Wait, payment is hard. Payment prototype also done by Nov 1st.
